Continues feature/anatomy-asset-safety-stage36 from 584fb3a05499a7e0887ae94e3fe2bf2456560517. Existing regions, body navigation, Smart Load, import, authentication, roles and user data are preserved. No schema changes, new runtime dependencies or production deployment.

## Real geometry

One on-demand back model is shared by lower_back_spine and upper_back_scapula. It contains 48 selectable structures, 5,531,004 bytes and 268,568 triangles. The 6 MiB cap accommodates original source geometry without cropping or simplification. Only one detailed asset and one WebGL scene are owned at a time; body navigation does not preload this asset. Real browser cleanup is checked.

- Midline: 24 individually mapped presacral vertebrae (C1–C7, T1–T12, L1–L5) and sacrum.
- Right attachment context: hip bone, scapula, clavicle and 12 ribs.
- Right muscles: trapezius (three original portions combined), rhomboid major/minor, iliocostalis lumborum/thoracis, longissimus thoracis, spinalis thoracis and semispinalis thoracis.

Right-side geometry is not mirrored. Vertebrae and sacrum are explicitly labeled midline. Default and Reset views are posterior. Bone/muscle layers, individual selection/highlighting, isolation, focus, camera presets and clear selection reuse the established viewer.

Unavailable: intervertebral discs, ligaments, cartilage, joint capsules, separate tendons, nerves, spinal cord, coccyx, skull, contralateral paired structures and unlisted muscles. Latissimus dorsi, multifidus and quadratus lumborum are explicitly unavailable geometry; their existing learning cards remain available. No fabricated geometry substitutes for missing structures. Cervical bones provide spinal context; the detailed neck region remains pending.
