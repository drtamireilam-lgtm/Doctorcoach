# Dedicated neck and cervical spine atlas

Continues feature/anatomy-asset-safety-stage36 from 8c746d0112b02112294b132e7dc3efe576a2363e. Existing atlas regions, Smart Load, imports, authentication, roles and user data remain. No database migration, dependency/lockfile change, main merge or Production deployment.

## Geometry and coverage

One on-demand neck asset: 32 selectable structures, 3,107,456 bytes and 145,676 triangles, admitted under a 3 MiB budget.

- Twenty selectable bones: seven cervical vertebrae, T1–T6 attachment context, occipital bone and combined sternum at midline; right temporal bone, clavicle, scapula and ribs1–2.
- Twelve selectable muscle entries: sternocleidomastoid, levator scapulae, scalenes (three original muscles grouped), longus capitis, splenius capitis/cervicis, semispinalis capitis, upper trapezius (original descending portion), rectus capitis posterior major/minor and obliquus capitis superior/inferior. Four suboccipital muscles are individually selectable.

All source geometry is retained. No cropping, mirroring or synthesis. Initial/Reset camera focuses on actual C4 bounds in anterior view; users can rotate, zoom out to attachment context, isolate/focus smaller muscles, toggle bones/muscles and clear selection. Only one owned scene is rendered; leaving releases GPU resources.

Visible coverage notes distinguish midline bones/right paired structures and partial skull context. Right longus colli is unavailable: official mapping exposes FJ1557, FJ1600 and FJ1601 only as left components, so none is mirrored or mislabeled as right. Discs, cartilage, ligaments, capsules, separately selectable tendons, nerves, vessels, spinal cord, complete skull and unlisted muscles are not included. Selection of an unmodeled learning entry does not highlight another mesh.
