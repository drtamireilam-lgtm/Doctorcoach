# Right hip and pelvis atlas extension

Continues feature/anatomy-asset-safety-stage36 from 718d117e92b20e244024b83d6555cd4c29d73bcb. Existing shoulder, arm, forearm, hand, body navigation, Smart Load, plan imports, authentication, roles and database data are preserved. No database migration or Production deployment.

## Available structures

One original right-side BodyParts3D subset, 749,044 bytes and 31,014 triangles. Two bones: right hip bone and femur. Twelve muscles: gluteus maximus, medius and minimus; tensor fasciae latae; piriformis; obturator internus and externus; superior and inferior gemellus; quadratus femoris; iliacus; psoas major.

The model opens from the Hip / glutes body region, accessible selector, or a muscle deep link. Bone and muscle meshes support raycast selection, named accessible selection, highlight, isolation, layers, presets and focus. Camera, region and muscle survive exercise navigation. Hebrew RTL and English LTR use the existing app.

This is a right hemipelvis and hip-muscle subset, not the entire pelvis or complete thigh. Sacrum/lumbar context bones, contralateral pelvis, separate tendons, iliotibial tract, cartilage, capsule, ligaments, nerves and unlisted muscles are unavailable and recorded in the public manifest. Existing anterior groin/adductor and knee regions remain explicitly without detailed models; they are not silently replaced by the gluteal model.

## Learning content

Reused the three gluteal cards, added innervation references, and completed nine cards for the other modeled muscles. Attachments, actions, joint context, innervation and training context are bilingual. References: UAMS lower-limb tables and individual University of Washington Muscle Atlas pages linked per card. TFL actions and usual gemelli nerve patterns were cross-checked against UAMS; UW documents gemelli innervation variation. Psoas innervation is described without claiming a uniform segment range. The erroneous adductor-magnus innervation text on UW's obturator externus page was not used: its card uses UAMS.

No copyrighted UW illustrations were copied. No new 2D illustration is fabricated. This is reference checking, not a documented clinician review; all assets remain marked clinical review pending.

Canonical exercise records are reused. Gluteus maximus links to hip thrust/bridge and other existing exercises; gluteus medius/minimus include single-leg balance as stabilizer. TFL assists hip abduction; piriformis assists clamshell with a joint-angle qualification. Iliacus/psoas link to active hanging leg raises. Other deep rotators explicitly have no dedicated exercise link; no parallel exercise database or false strengthening role for stretches was added.
