## Geometry and movement

All 15 records have bilateral selectable volume proxies, not sculpted anatomical shapes. Absolute size, contour, depth, relationships, pennation, heads/tendons and attachment landmarks are not validated. Frame is a body silhouette proxy, NOT an anatomical skeleton. Deep structures can overlap and can be obscured; turn off the superficial layer to inspect them.

Arm segments rotate around approximate shoulder pivots. Deltoid/cuff/trunk shapes remain fixed. There is no tendon excursion, muscle deformation, humeral axial rotation, scapular upward rotation, clavicular movement, impingement model or force/EMG calculation. Serratus anterior is missing from the requested first subset and its essential scapular contribution is explicitly mentioned in the UI. The 0–90 degree movement illustrates controls and rigid transforms only. Do not use it to teach exact mechanics or prescribe rehabilitation.
