## Geometry and interaction

Original BodyParts3D right knee subset: 1,079,544 bytes and 52,952 triangles, with 16 selectable structures from 17 source ELEMENTs.

Four joint bones: femur, patella, tibia and fibula. Right hip bone and calcaneus provide muscle-attachment context. Ten muscles: rectus femoris, vastus lateralis, vastus medialis, vastus intermedius, biceps femoris long and short heads separately, semitendinosus, semimembranosus, popliteus, and gastrocnemius (two original heads combined).

Original whole muscles and bones are retained. Initial camera and reset/presets frame the knee using the actual patella bounds with a fourfold context scale; zooming out reveals full attachment context. The framing does not crop, cut or synthesize geometry. Focus can show a full selected muscle; stored user camera is restored after default framing. Existing models retain their prior defaults.

Body knee selection, accessible mesh list, raycast picker, highlight, isolation, layers, presets, focus and bilingual information reuse the shared viewer. No new animation loop or external model URL. One scene at a time and existing disposal remain in place.

Menisci, cruciate/collateral ligaments, cartilage, capsule, separate tendons, nerves and unlisted muscles are unavailable. The viewer explicitly discloses the missing knee structures and labels context bones. This is not a complete ligamentous knee or entire thigh/calf/foot model.
