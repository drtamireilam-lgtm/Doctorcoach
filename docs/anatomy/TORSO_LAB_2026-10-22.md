Continues feature/anatomy-asset-safety-stage36 from ff55db61b146367902b0b5661bd2f480f946cb36. The existing application, earlier anatomical regions, Smart Load, imports, permissions and user data are preserved. No database migration, dependency or lockfile change. LAB only; no main merge.

## Available source geometry

One lazy-loaded model shared by chest and core_abdomen: 28 selectable structures, 6,186,000 bytes, 315,796 triangles, a 6 MiB admission budget. Original geometry is retained without simplification, mirroring or cropping. Only one model/scene is owned at a time; browser acceptance checks release of the WebGL context on leaving.

- Midline sternum combines original manubrium, sternal body and xiphoid.
- Right bones: 12 ribs, clavicle, scapula, humerus and hip bone. The latter four provide muscle-attachment context. Seventeen selectable bones total.
- Right costal cartilages 1–7, each independently selectable and in a distinct cartilage layer.
- Right muscles: pectoralis major (three original source parts combined), pectoralis minor, serratus anterior and external oblique.

Coverage is explicitly partial in a visible note above the viewer, in detailed availability text and in source metadata. Rectus abdominis, internal oblique and transversus abdominis have no geometry in this selected subset; their existing learning cards remain reachable without highlighting another muscle. Intercostal muscles, diaphragm, internal organs, nerves, vessels, ligaments, joint capsules, separate tendons, costal cartilages 8–10 and contralateral paired structures are not included. No complete abdominal musculature or organ-system atlas is claimed.

Existing rotation/pinch/zoom, presets, Reset, focus, selection/highlight, isolation, clear selection and camera persistence apply. Cartilage has its own bilingual control and blue-gray base color rather than being labeled muscle or bone. Sternum selection explicitly says Midline. Shared chest/abdomen navigation preserves originating region and muscle when returning from exercise.

## Content and exercise relationships

All four muscles reuse canonical bilingual learning cards with attachments, actions, joints and training context. Pectoralis minor and external oblique gain reference-checked innervation. UAMS thorax tables support medial pectoral nerve C8–T1; primary external-oblique innervation research supports segmental supply. Academic abdominal-wall tables and publisher material were checked for lower thoracic nerve supply. Variability is qualified rather than claiming uniform individual anatomy.

- https://medicine.uams.edu/neuroscience/education/medical-school-courses/human-structure-module/anatomy-tables/muscle-tables/muscles-of-the-thorax/
- https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/11-4-axial-muscles-of-the-abdominal-wall-and-thorax
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10409811/
- https://anatomy.ttuhscep.edu/schemes/abdo_wall_tables.html

No proprietary reference images/models were copied. No direct visual Netter/Sobotta comparison or clinical approval is claimed. Clinical review remains pending.

Bench variations reuse primary pectoralis-major links; wall slide reuses serratus anterior; woodchop and side plank reuse external oblique. Side plank correctly displays an isometric stabilizer role. Pectoralis minor explicitly has no targeted exercise link rather than inventing one. Canonical exercise records are not duplicated.
