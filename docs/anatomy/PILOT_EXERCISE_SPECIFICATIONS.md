## Biceps Curl · barbell-curl

The LAB player demonstrates standing bilateral elbow flexion and controlled return. The source posture and forearm orientation remain fixed outside the elbow transform. There is no authored supination or finger grip, no represented bar and no shoulder compensation. This is a partial motion study for the catalog entry.

Atlas references are bilateral biceps brachii and brachialis (primary), brachioradialis (assisting), and flexor carpi radialis and ulnaris (provisional stabilization examples). The same role map applies throughout the cycle. The mesh colors appear in the source-neutral reference. Completion of the loaded variant requires verified hand/grip orientation, a rigid-bar constraint and review of the pivots and chosen range.

## Bench Press · barbell-bench

Target variant is a flat barbell press, with a supported supine trunk and feet. Start with the hands above the shoulder region, lower the bar toward the chest and return to a stable top pose. Calibrate shoulder horizontal motion, elbow flexion/extension, scapular behavior and a continuous two-hand grip on one bar. Inspect bar clearance and bench support in side and oblique views. Provisional content topics are pectoralis major, anterior deltoid and triceps.

The inherited LAB clip is an illustrative flat-press preview. Interactive playback awaits source-calibrated shoulder and elbow coupling, an explicit supine setup and bench contact, and grip constraints. The asset has neither skinning nor muscle attachments for a deforming display.

## Squat · back-squat

Target variant is a high-bar back squat. Start standing with a stable stance, descend through coordinated hip, knee and ankle flexion and return to standing. Record bar support and hand position, maintain planted feet and describe any balance approximation. Inspect side and oblique views for floor clearance, foot sliding, bar drift and knee/hip alignment. Provisional content topics are quadriceps, gluteus maximus, adductors and trunk stabilizers. Forward knee travel depends on the demonstrated technique and anatomy.

The asset lacks a linked lower-limb rig, bilateral foot constraints, validated patellar tracking and a bar-support pose. Separate atlas joint demonstrations do not supply the coordinated motion required for this pilot.
