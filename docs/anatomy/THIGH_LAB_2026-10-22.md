# Thigh and groin atlas — 2026-10-22

Extends the existing DoctorCoach LAB atlas with one shared thigh/groin model, loaded only when opened. Body navigator entries for both regions resolve to this model. Existing shoulder, arm, hand, hip, knee, calf, foot, back, chest and neck models remain unchanged. The head remains navigation context only; other regions may still have partial coverage.

## Available geometry

Five original right bones: hip bone, femur, patella, tibia and fibula. Fourteen individually selectable right muscles: rectus femoris, vastus lateralis/medialis/intermedius, biceps femoris long/short heads, semitendinosus, semimembranosus, sartorius, pectineus, adductor longus/brevis/magnus and gracilis. Nineteen meshes, 61,986 triangles, 1,317,296 bytes, below the 3MiB runtime ceiling.

Initial camera uses actual adductor-magnus bounds to frame the thigh; full tibia/fibula geometry remains available as attachment context. Rotation, pinch/wheel zoom, fixed views, accessible selection, highlight, isolate, focus, clear and bone/muscle layers reuse the existing viewer. Back from an exercise restores the originating region, selection and camera. Canonical reverse quadriceps links now open thigh; starting from knee and pressing Back still retains knee context.

Adductor magnus is one source mesh; functional portions are explained in text without invented separate geometry. No separate tendons, ligaments, nerves, cartilage, capsules, vessels or contralateral structures are included. Iliopsoas components and obturator externus remain available in the hip model. No source is mirrored or cropped.
