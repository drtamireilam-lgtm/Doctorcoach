## Version 13 — within-workout RPE feedback
- Each set shows a live RIR-adjusted Epley e1RM estimate; supported range is loaded repetitions 1–10 and RPE 6–10. It is labeled an estimate, not a measured maximum. Conceptual source: Helms et al., 2016, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4961270/. Combining RIR with Epley is an application heuristic; individual accuracy is not asserted.
- After saving a completed set, the immediately following set receives a calculated load suggestion for its repetitions and the coach's target RPE. Equipment increments are selectable. A product guardrail caps each suggested change at 5%, rounding toward the current weight. This cap is not presented as a research-derived clinical threshold.
- Applying a suggestion is an explicit button action, recalculated on the server and persisted only to the next unperformed set. Existing version checks, ownership and medical-review training guards apply. Other draft edits must be saved first. Completed sets and the future workout plan are not changed.
- Pain reported before/after the session suppresses suggestions; in-set guidance tells the trainee to stop and contact the team for pain or technique deterioration.
- Team progress includes active-session and recent per-set RPE/e1RM. CSV export preserves the original estimate and adds a distinct RPE-adjusted estimate column.
- 58 tests pass, including direction/rounding, missing data, pain, out-of-range RPE, persistence, ownership, stale writes and completed-set protection.

## Version 14 — RIR preference

## Version 15 — coached and independent access
- Default access is coached. Only active, linked staff with role coach can assign an executable coaching program. Personal plan mutation, templates and exercise swaps require an active independent membership, enforced in the stores and APIs. Coach-owned program snapshots are the source for coached workouts; editing old program_items cannot change them. RPE/RIR, repetitions performed, actual loads and next-set load recommendations remain available.
- Independent membership has an owner-configured price/payment link or instructions, explicit purchase request, payment report, admin verification, calendar-month expiry and manual renewal. No price was invented, no actual charge was made and recurring billing/webhooks are not connected. The default catalog is closed until configured.
- Approved payments carry unique references, immutable price snapshots and version checks. Reports alone never grant access. Renewal extends the remaining paid term. Users can return to a still-valid paid independent membership without paying again after a coached interval.
- Mode/program changes require finishing or cancelling the active session. Expiry or coach revocation blocks new set activity; finishing/cancelling an existing session and reading history remain possible. Old pre-policy workouts are not upgraded into coached programs.
- Personal plans and history are preserved; no destructive migration. User export includes their own membership records. Actual Nadav account setup still requires his verified sign-in email in the existing staff settings; no identity or invite was fabricated.
