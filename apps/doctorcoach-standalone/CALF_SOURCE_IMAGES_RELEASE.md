# Calf source illustrations — 2026-10-10

Three unchanged public-domain Gray’s Anatomy plates now appear in seven matched muscle records. Total coverage is seven source images for nineteen records.

- Gray437: tibialis anterior and fibularis tertius (historical label: peroneus tertius). Commons derivative descriptions identify Gray437 as their source.
- Gray438: soleus and plantaris, identified by the source plate and Commons categories.
- Gray439: tibialis posterior, flexor digitorum longus and flexor hallucis longus. Commons derivative records identify the corresponding plate.

Original source, attribution, license, intrinsic dimensions and original-size links remain visible. No generated anatomy or blanket region assignment. Gastrocnemius and unmatched neighboring muscles remain unassigned.
